📖 How to Play

The Deck (56 cards)
🂠52 standard cards (A–K × ♥♦♣♠) · 2 Health Cards · 1 Red Joker · 1 Black Joker
📐Ranks: A=1, 2–10, J=11, Q=12, K=13
Your Turn — Guess the Next Card
🔢Number card showing (A–10) — tap Higher, Lower, or Joker
👑Face card showing (J, Q, K) — tap Red, Black, or Joker
Correct Guess
✅Choose Pass (next player goes) or Continue guessing on the same turn.
Wrong Guess — Punishment!
❌Wrong guess → 1 push-up, keep guessing until you're right.
🟰Same number → 2 push-ups
🎯Same number and same color → 3 push-ups
💚 Health Cards
💚Drawn like any card — no penalty. You keep it.
1️⃣Hold 1: tap Skip to avoid any push-up once (card removed).
2️⃣Hold 2: Skip −1 (same) or Flip −2 to peek the next card (both cards shuffled back).
🃏 Jokers
✅Correct Joker guess → all other players do 2 push-ups. Joker set aside. You continue.
❌Wrong Joker guess or surprise Joker → you do 2 push-ups. Joker set aside. Next player's turn.
⚡ Challenge
⚡Tap Challenge before guessing to dare another player to guess for you.
🏆They guess right → you do 3 push-ups and lose your turn.
💀They guess wrong → they do 1 push-up, your turn resumes.
