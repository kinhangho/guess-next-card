How to Play

THE RULES ARE SIMPLE,
THE PRESSURE ISN'T
  • Number card up? Guess Higher, Lower, or Joker.
  • Face card up? Guess Red, Black, or Joker.
  • Right guess: pass the phone or keep going.
  • Wrong guess: pay the penalty, then guess again.
IT ESCALATES FAST
  • Heat climbs every time someone pushes their luck instead of passing — and it doesn't reset until somebody misses. Whoever that is pays for everyone's confidence.
  • Not feeling a card? Challenge someone else to guess in your place. They call it wrong and you're in the clear. They call it right and you're covering triple.
  • Guess Joker correctly and the whole table pays but you. Get caught off guard by one and you're the only one paying.
  • Health Cards turn up at random — hold one to dodge a penalty, hold two to sneak a peek at what's coming next.
SET YOUR OWN STAKES
  • Before you start, pick what losing costs: push-ups, sit-ups, squats, a timed plank, or write your own.
  • Everyone picks an avatar — snap a photo or grab one of 30 emoji — and a live scoreboard keeps score of who's losing the hardest.
The Deck (56 cards)
52 standard cards (A–K × ♥♦♣♠) · 2 Health Cards · 1 Red Joker · 1 Black Joker
Ranks: A=1, 2–10, J=11, Q=12, K=13
Your Turn — Guess the Next Card
Number card showing (A–10) — tap Higher, Lower, or Joker
Face card showing (J, Q, K) — tap Red, Black, or Joker
Correct Guess
Choose Pass (next player goes) or Continue guessing on the same turn.
Wrong Guess — Punishment!
Wrong guess -> 1 push-up, keep guessing until you're right.
Same number -> 2 push-ups
Same number and same color -> 3 push-ups
Health Cards
Drawn like any card — no penalty. You keep it.
Hold 1: tap Skip to avoid any push-up once (card removed).
Hold 2: Skip −1 (same) or Flip −2 to peek the next card (both cards shuffled back).
Jokers
Correct Joker guess -> all other players do 2 push-ups. Joker set aside. You continue.
Wrong Joker guess or surprise Joker -> you do 2 push-ups. Joker set aside. Next player's turn.
Challenge
Tap Challenge before guessing to dare another player to guess for you.
They guess right -> you do 3 push-ups and lose your turn.
They guess wrong -> they do 1 push-up, your turn resumes.
